Maintenance and reapplication

Plan maintenance against documentation and use

Follow current product guidance for cleaning and maintenance. Inspection or reapplication needs must be assessed for the specific surface, cleaning routine, and operating load.

Review documentationRequest technical review
Maintenance plan

Confirm with the responsible specialist

Cleaning

Check compatible cleaning agents, methods, and any limits in current guidance.

Inspection

Agree how wear, damage, and surface changes will be recorded.

Reapplication

Use current product data, actual condition, and project requirements to assess need.

Do not assume a universal lifetime or fixed service interval without current documentation for the correct product version and use.
