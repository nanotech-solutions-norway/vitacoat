Provenance and identity

Current VitaCoat naming and historical Citrox sources must remain distinct

Current name

VitaCoat is the approved current product name and NanoTech Solutions Norway AS is the current product/company attribution in internal registers.

Historical reports

Citrox/Citrox Protect and Citrox Biosciences Ltd may appear in original third-party reports. That metadata must be preserved and must not be rewritten in issued reports.

Claim gate

Formulation equivalence, report rebranding/reassignment, current SDS/regulatory status, and public claim scope remain PENDING_REVIEW and require controlled approval.

Using standards

The standard describes the method — the report describes the tested sample

ReferenceEvidence roleControl before use
EN 13727 / EN 1276Bacteria-related test methodology in historical/updated report sets.Confirm report ID, sample, contact time, conditions, and current product linkage.
EN 13624 / EN 1650Yeast/fungal-related test methodology in separate report sets.Do not generalize a result from one sample or campaign to another without documented support.

Technical decision

What should be confirmed before external use

Document match

  • Report ID and issuing laboratory
  • Sample/batch identity
  • Test standard and contact time
  • Date and test conditions
  • Original client/sponsor and signatures

Current product match

  • Documented formulation equivalence where required
  • Current SDS/TDS and product status
  • Approved public claim scope
  • Substrate and use environment
  • Project-specific technical review
