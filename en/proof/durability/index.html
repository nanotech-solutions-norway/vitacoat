Durability

Durability must be assessed for the intended use

There is no single documented service interval for every VitaCoat use case. Review current product documentation, representative surfaces, cleaning chemistry, wear, and inspection before setting a maintenance plan.

What to verify

Link test results to the product and operating environment

Report and sample

Check the report ID, sample, test setup, and link to the current product version.

Operating conditions

Assess substrate, cleaning chemistry, touch exposure, friction, and mechanical wear.

Follow-up

Set inspection and any reapplication from a documented pilot and current guidance.

Historical test results describe the samples and conditions stated in their reports. They do not establish a universal lifetime for the current product.
