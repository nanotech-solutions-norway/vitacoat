How VitaCoat should be evaluated

A transparent SiO₂-based surface layer with controlled evidence provenance

VitaCoat is the current marketed product name. For hard, non-porous surfaces, technical evaluation should rely on current product documentation, substrate compatibility, and the specific test report supporting the intended claim.

Current product identity

VitaCoat and the SiO₂ matrix

VitaCoat

VitaCoat is the approved current product name. Citrox, Citrox Protect, and Citrox Protect Hard Surface Sealant should be treated as historical aliases and search terms when tracing legacy documentation.

SiO₂-based surface layer

The product is described as a transparent SiO₂-based surface layer for suitable hard, non-porous substrates. Surface fit, preparation, and compatibility should be confirmed for the actual application.

Evidence provenance

Historical documentation must not be rewritten into new company or formulation evidence

2020 reports

EN 13727, EN 13624, and EN 14476 exist in historical Citrox-/VitaCoat-labelled derivatives sharing study or report references. Original report IDs, results, samples, and laboratory metadata should be preserved.

2022 campaign

Updated Citrox reports from 2022 use new sample and report identifiers and should be treated as a separate evidence set. Overlapping PASS conclusions do not by themselves establish formulation equivalence.

Current use

Whether historical reports support a current VitaCoat specification, claim, or regulatory context must be confirmed through technical and documentary review.

Original third-party provenance must be preserved. Historical Citrox documents are evidence, not authority to alter the issuing laboratory, sponsor, client, sample, or reported results.
Operational integration

Supplements cleaning — does not replace it

Surface fit

Confirm that the substrate is hard, non-porous, and compatible with the intended preparation and application process.

Cleaning regime

Routine cleaning and disinfection remain fundamental hygiene measures. Compatible chemistry and mechanical load should be included in project review.

Service interval

Maintenance and reapplication should be set project-by-project according to use, cleaning, wear, and controlled technical documentation.

Technical clarification

Need to connect historical test evidence to a current VitaCoat project?

Request a technical review of report ID, sample, standard, contact time, substrate, cleaning regime, and document status before specification or procurement.
