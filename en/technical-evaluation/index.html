Technical evaluation & procurement

A decision path from surface fit to documented procurement basis

This page is for technical evaluators, procurement, operations/HSE and project owners who need a clear sequence for qualifying VitaCoat before pilot, specification or rollout.

Request Technical ReviewOpen Documentation
Decision gates

Six questions to resolve before procurement

01

Is the substrate within the normal fit?

Confirm a hard, non-porous surface and identify coatings, finishes or material characteristics that require separate compatibility review.

02

What is the hygiene objective?

Define VitaCoat as a supplementary hygienic surface layer and retain existing cleaning and disinfection protocols.

03

Is the evidence relevant?

Connect the intended claim to the correct test method, organism, contact time, substrate and laboratory conditions.

04

What affects the service window?

Review abrasion, cleaning chemistry, touch frequency, maintenance and application quality.

05

Which documents are required?

Define TDS, SDS, test evidence, application guidance and any internal regulatory or procurement documentation.

06

Is a pilot required?

Use a scoped pilot where substrate, operating load or internal approval requires site-specific validation.

What an evaluator should request

Safety Data Sheet (SDS)Handling, classification, protective measures and relevant chemical safety information.HSE, operations and internal chemical approval.
Test reports / method basisTest organism, standard, conditions, contact time and measured result.When performance claims are used in the decision basis.

Project data

Information that improves technical review

Surface & environment

  • Material/substrate and finish
  • Indoor/outdoor operating environment
  • Touch frequency and mechanical wear

Cleaning & operations

  • Cleaning/disinfection chemistry
  • Frequency and method
  • Any abrasive processes

Decision needs

  • Claims that must be evidenced
  • Required internal documentation
  • Timeline, pilot size and decision point
Decision roles

Different functions review the same product differently

Technical / engineering

Substrate fit, mechanism, test methods, application and limitations.

Operations / hygiene

Cleaning routines, touch load, maintenance and service interval.

HSE / quality

SDS, risk assessment, document control and approved working procedures.

Procurement

Document completeness, delivery/pilot scope and controlled specification basis.

The website is an orientation and decision-support layer. Final product selection and specification should be based on controlled documentation and project-specific technical review.
