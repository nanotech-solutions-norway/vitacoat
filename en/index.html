How it works

Three mechanisms within one persistent surface layer

VitaCoat combines antimicrobial function with surface characteristics and a bonded SiO₂ matrix. The mechanisms should be assessed together and within the documented application scope.

01

Antimicrobial contact function

The active chemistry is designed to reduce microbial burden when relevant organisms contact the treated surface.

02

Surface character and anti-adhesion

The treated surface gains characteristics that support reduced adhesion and easier maintenance on suitable hard, non-porous substrates.

03

Bonded persistent layer

The SiO₂ matrix anchors the functional layer to the surface so hygienic support can persist between routine cleaning intervals.

The protection gap between scheduled cleaning events

Cleaning and disinfection remain fundamental. VitaCoat is designed as a supplementary layer during the period between those events.
