Cleaning and maintenance

Assess cleaning performance on the intended surface

This site does not confirm that VitaCoat reduces cleaning time, chemical use, or wear. Assess these outcomes against current product documentation and a controlled test using relevant soil, cleaning chemistry, and method.

Request technical reviewReview documentation
Evaluation approach

Compare under consistent conditions

Select representative surfaces

Record material, finish, preparation, and any existing surface treatment.

Use the same cleaning method

Record agent, concentration, cloth or pad, pressure, and number of passes.

Measure relevant outcomes

Agree how cleaning time, visible residue, surface change, and function will be assessed.

Limitations

Do not generalize without evidence

Results from one surface, cleaning agent, or test method do not automatically apply to other materials and operating conditions. Continue routine cleaning and disinfection.
