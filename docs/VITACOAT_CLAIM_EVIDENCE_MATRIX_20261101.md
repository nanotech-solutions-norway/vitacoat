## Cross-page risk queue

1. **Continuous/persistent protection and six-month language:** map each phrasing to a specific report/sample, contact cycle, wear conditions, maintenance interval, and current formulation. A simulated touch or hydrophobicity result cannot be restated as current antimicrobial service life without documented linkage and approval.
2. **Healthcare and clinical wording:** separate laboratory efficacy from clinical use, infection prevention, transmission, safety, and suitability claims. No clinical outcome or infection-prevention guarantee is inferred.
3. **Food processing:** retain the site's stated boundary against direct food/ingredient contact unless an exact separate authorization/evidence set is produced. Do not infer food-safe status from historic assay references.
4. **Public facilities, education/offices, electronics:** map operational-benefit statements to evidence, surface/material scope and cleaning conditions. Do not promote cleaning reduction or passive continuous effect as verified performance without applicable proof.
5. **Pathogen spectrum:** maintain organism-by-organism, strain, sample, method, contact-time and condition mapping. Literature or ingredient-level evidence does not establish finished-formulation performance.
6. **Safety/environment and regulatory statements:** technical substantiation and jurisdiction-specific regulatory review are separate gates; no REACH/RoHS, non-toxic, hypoallergenic, VOC or biocidal status is approved by this matrix.
