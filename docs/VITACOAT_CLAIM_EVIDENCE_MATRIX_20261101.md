## Priority risk queue

1. **Continuous/persistent protection and six-month language:** reconcile each phrasing against report/sample, contact cycle, wear conditions, maintenance interval and current formulation. Simulated touch/hydrophobicity findings do not establish current antimicrobial service life without linkage and approval.
2. **Healthcare/clinical wording:** separate laboratory efficacy from clinical use, infection prevention, transmission, safety and suitability. No clinical outcome or infection-prevention guarantee is inferred.
3. **Food processing:** retain the stated boundary against direct food/ingredient contact unless separate evidence and authorization exist. Do not infer food-safe status from historic assay references.
