**Subject:** Current VitaCoat Hard Surfaces — controlled formulation identity, evidence bridge and approved safety/technical documents

> Dear Responsible Manufacturer / Technical and Regulatory Records Team,
>
> NanoTech Solutions Norway AS is completing its controlled release and evidence qualification for **VitaCoat – Hard Surfaces**, the SiO₂-based hard-surface coating product line. We request a dated, signed manufacturer/regulatory statement for the **currently supplied finished formulation**, not a generic historical description or a differently marketed coating.
>
> **Product identity and traceability.** Please identify the current legal manufacturer/formulator, applicable product trade name, version, finished SKU, batch/lot identification rules, country of manufacture, actual intended commercial markets and whether current production is active. Confirm whether your manufacturing entity differs from the one identified on the historical **01 June 2022 Citrox Protect Hard Surface Sealant** SDS. If you are not the competent current custodian, please identify the role/organization responsible.
>
> **Historical equivalence.** We hold primary historic studies on sample `20D/103` (2020), `22C/071` (2022 ethanol variant), and wear-study batch `2007165-CP`. For each, please provide either (a) a signed formulation and use-condition bridging conclusion, including what chemical/material changes have occurred and why a specific study remains applicable, or (b) an explicit **NO EQUIVALENCE ESTABLISHED** statement. Do not assume that a shared Citrox/VitaCoat brand or historic marketing authorization constitutes the needed bridge.
>
> **SDS/CLP.** Supply the latest formally **approved** EU/EEA/Norway Safety Data Sheet under REACH Annex II as amended by Regulation (EU) **2020/878**, with document control, issue/revision date and authorized signatory. Confirm actual CLP hazard classes, pictograms, signal word, H/P statements, language requirements, current ingredient/hazard calculations and any relevant nanoform identification. The draft `VC-HS-SDS-EN-1.2` dated **10 November 2026** is marked **NOT APPROVED FOR RELEASE** and retains unresolved current-mixture classifications. Please document a reconciled version-control trail for the 23 August and 10 November 2026 drafts. Do **not** replicate a historic flammable/irritation classification without evaluating the actual present mixture.
>
> **UFI / poison-centre and other reporting.** Identify whether CLP Annex VIII notification and UFI requirements apply to the actual mixture and intended markets/uses, with documented justification if not. If applicable, give the verified current UFI and poison-centre notification/PCN status **only through a protected authorized channel**, not a public repository. Clarify relevant Norwegian Product Register obligations, REACH substance data, SVHC/restriction screening and occupational exposure controls.
>
> **Biocidal status.** Disclose through the restricted compliance process the identities and regulatory roles of any functional active substances, target microorganisms, modes of action and relevant active/substance/product type data. Provide Article 95 supplier evidence and applicable BPR authorization, application or transition legal basis if claims confer a biocidal function. If the product relies on a non-biocidal purely physical mechanism, give the evidence and qualified regulatory justification. Please distinguish the coating mixture sold for application from any separately marketed **treated article**.
>
> **Approved TDS and IFU.** Supply versioned, approved technical data and instructions covering permitted substrates and intended uses, mixing/dilution, cleaning/preparation, application method and rate, minimum wet/dry film specification if applicable, cure/dry conditions, ventilation/PPE, storage/shelf life, postapplication cleaning, abrasion compatibility and conditions limiting claims. Clearly identify variants or solvent/water substitutions and disallow unsupported extrapolations from alternative formulas.
>
> **Antimicrobial evidence after coating.** Provide any signed **finished cured-film** efficacy studies (specimen coating application, target microorganisms, suitable surface test standard, inoculation/contact time, cleaning/abrasion and aging exposure). Distinguish immediate suspension assays from postcuring surface effects; give explicit evidence for or exclusion of any proposed **six-month antimicrobial duration** statement.
>
> Please provide a short, signed decision table identifying for each required section: `APPROVED`, `NOT APPLICABLE` with reason, `PENDING`, or `NOT AVAILABLE`; the exact document number, revision, authorizing role, application-market scope and any usage restrictions. Proprietary composition and contract information should be transmitted only through an approved secure channel after recipient authentication.
>
> We will not interpret historical reports or any draft document as current regulatory approval until these items are verified.
>
