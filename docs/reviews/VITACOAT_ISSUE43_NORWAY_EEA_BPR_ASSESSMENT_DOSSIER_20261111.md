## 3. Required closed-form decisions from the qualified assessor
The assessor should sign a controlled decision record answering the following **for the exact currently marketed finished formulation and intended usage**:
1. **Biocidal action** — Is any constituent an active substance for the intended antimicrobial function? Identify relevant CAS/EC IDs through secure controlled channels and mechanism of action; is the intended effect chemically/biologically biocidal or purely physical/mechanical, with citations?
2. **Object and claim** — Is the sold coating mixture a biocidal product under Article 3? Is each coated downstream object a treated article, a biocidal product with primary function, or outside the scope for a specific use?
3. **PT and market** — Which Annex V PT applies to each commercial application (e.g., PT2 candidate only); which territory is covered (Norway/EEA member markets)? Are food-contact, clinical, healthcare, veterinary/aquaculture or other regulated uses explicitly excluded?
4. **Legally marketable now** — What authorization/transition pathway applies, and what official product/active identifiers, approvals, authorisation holder, approval period, conditions, Article 95 listed supplier and transitional deadlines establish compliance? If these cannot be identified, mark `NO CURRENT MARKETING BASIS VERIFIED`.
5. **Labels and advertising** — Which Article 58 disclosures (when applicable), Article 72 restrictions (where applicable), mandatory Norwegian text, allowed public statements, cleaning/safe-use instructions, nanoform disclosure or other hazard labeling are required?
6. **SDS and notification** — Confirm current CLP/hazard classifications, approved SDS revision in market languages, required UFI/PCN/poison-centre actions if applicable, professional-use restrictions and substance/nanoform screening.
7. **Durability and qualified efficacy** — Which tests of the **current cured coating** at claimed age and after defined washing/abrasion establish each public efficacy phrase? Historical suspension tests from 2020 and 2022 or 5,000-touch physical hydrophobicity do not by themselves support a continuous six-month antimicrobial effect.
8. **Publisher and rights** — Which issuer/sponsor consents authorize quoting original versus revised reports, lab logos, excerpts and exact modified sponsor names? Which revised materials may be used without violating original-report limitations?
9. **Decision output** — Provide qualified reviewer's full name/role and authorization, report revision/date, source IDs stored privately, market-by-market determination, specific claim matrix with exact NO/EN wording and permitted scope, outstanding exclusions and required remediation. State `APPROVED / APPROVED_WITH_CONDITIONS / NOT_APPROVED / FURTHER_EVIDENCE_NEEDED` per claim and jurisdiction.
